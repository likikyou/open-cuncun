# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Cuncun Core is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point
of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas, code, and documentation.
- Give actionable feedback and accept reasonable disagreement.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and project boundaries.
- Help newcomers participate without intimidation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or sustained off-topic hostility.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing
the project, including Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `likikyou999@gmail.com`. Do not open a public Issue
for a sensitive report. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as
practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules fairly and explain enforcement decisions
when doing so would not compromise anyone's privacy or safety.
